Core Features

Dr. MediLink — AI Doctor

  • Bangla, English and Banglish with a natural physician tone and follow-up questions.
  • Flash = concise; Pro = detailed, structured (Premium). Streaming, stop, copy, listen and voice input.
  • Local chat history, auto titles, delete, mobile drawer; free plan 2 chats/day.

Safety & Handover

  • Tiered emergency/serious/mental-health/minor/general detection; red flags point to 999.
  • Serious cases avoid medicine names; handover never invents a diagnosis or dose.
  • Editable doctor summary with red flags, specialty match, WhatsApp preview and Premium PDF.

Pharmacy

  • 12 demo medicines, search/filter/sort, stock-aware cart, ৳30 delivery, mobile cart bar.
  • bKash, Nagad, card or COD; simulated 1.7s success; order ID + WhatsApp handoff.
  • Rx-only gate, OCR prescription match, pharmacist-review status, 20% Premium discount.

Doctors & Emergency

  • 6 doctor profiles, search, specialty filters, nearby geolocation and distance sorting.
  • WhatsApp booking/video request, optional chat summary, 20% Premium fee discount.
  • 999 SOS, 5 hospitals, GPS/area fallback, road-time matrix, directions, SMS/WhatsApp location share.

Medication Reminders

  • Next dose, today timeline, all schedules; one-tap taken/skip/pause/resume.
  • Dose parsing from AI replies; schedule editor with times, duration and notes.
  • Toast + browser notification + service-worker notification; active count in chat sidebar.

Premium & Platform

  • ৳500/month or ৳4,800/year; bKash, Nagad, card; always-successful demo checkout.
  • Receipt, renewal date, active dashboard; Pro unlock, 20% pharmacy/doctor discounts, PDF.
  • Next.js 16.4.2, React 19.3.1, Turbopack, 3 free model pool, 4 APIs, SEO/JSON-LD, 115 tests.

Demo Boundaries — say this honestly on stage

No real auth/DB/encryption · profile, chat and reminders are local; payments and Premium are simulated · static doctor/medicine/hospital data; WhatsApp/SMS handoff, not real booking/delivery · OCR and Maps need API keys · free models can be slow; AI is assistive, not a diagnosis. In an emergency, call 999.
